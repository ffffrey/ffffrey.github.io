

<strong>Group leader:</strong>
<br>
Dr. Felix Frey is an Assistant Professor in the <a href="https://www.tue.nl/en/research/research-groups/soft-matter-and-biological-physics">Soft Matter and Biological Physics Group</a> in the <a href="https://www.tue.nl/en/our-university/departments/applied-physics-and-science-education">Department of Applied Physics and Science Education</a> at <a href="https://www.tue.nl/en/">Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)</a>.
Before joining TU/e, he was an independent <a href="https://nomisfoundation.ch/people/felix-frey/">NOMIS Fellow</a> at <a href="https://ist.ac.at/home">IST Austria</a> near Vienna, where he worked with <a href="https://andelasaric.com">Anđela Šarić</a>, and a postdoctoral researcher in the <a href="https://www.tudelft.nl/en/faculty-of-applied-sciences/about-faculty/departments/bionanoscience/">Department of Bionanoscience</a> at <a href="https://www.tudelft.nl/en/">Delft University of Technology</a> with <a href="https://www.idemalab.tudelft.nl/">Timon Idema</a>.
